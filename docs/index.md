# Welcome to the Backup Basics Handbook

Hey. Settle in — this is the friend-version of the talk about not losing your
stuff, the one that ends with you never finding out what "unsaved work" grief
feels like.

By the end of this handbook you'll have three things:

1. A small pile of backups that actually exist (not ones you plan to make).
2. A rhythm you can keep — ten minutes here and there, not a second job.
3. Proof that you can get your stuff **back**, because that's the whole point.

## Who this is for

You, if you have photos, documents, tax paperwork, a half-written novel, game
screenshots, kid photos, a spreadsheet that runs your whole life — and you'd
rather keep them than re-grieve them. No tech background assumed. Every word
is explained like a friend would: plain English, step by step, never
condescending.

## How to use this book

You have two options:

- **The one-evening path.** Read chapters 1–3, do the worksheet, copy your
  most important folder onto a drive tonight. That alone puts you ahead of
  most people.
- **The chapter-a-week path.** One chapter, one small action, then close the
  laptop. Slow and steady totally works here.

Every chapter ends with a **Do this today** box. Those boxes are the book.
Reading about backups does nothing; one ten-minute action does everything.

## The one-sentence version

If it would hurt to lose it, it needs at least two copies on two different
things, and one of those things needs to live somewhere else — and once a
season, you should practice getting a file back.

That's it. That's the whole book. The rest is the friendly details.
