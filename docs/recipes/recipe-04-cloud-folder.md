# Recipe 4 — Put a Folder in the Cloud

**Goal:** one cloud copy of your small-but-precious files — documents,
scans, budget spreadsheets — maintained by the app so you don't have to
remember anything. **You need:** a free cloud account and 10 minutes.

Small personal files fit comfortably in free tiers (documents are tiny;
photos are the bulky things). That's why this recipe is about *documents*.

## The steps (any provider — Google Drive, OneDrive, Dropbox, iCloud Drive)

1. **Pick one provider** — often already attached to your email or phone
   (that's fine, use it).
2. Install the provider's little app on your computer, or use the web
   page's upload.
3. Make a cloud folder named the same as your treasure folder
   (`my-treasures`), so future-you never translates between names.
4. Copy your **documents** (PDFs, text files, spreadsheets, scans — pick
   the ten-year formats from chapter 6's table) into it.
5. Let the app finish — the icon says "synced," with a little green or a
   checkmark or a calm cloud. Different providers, same smugness.

**Photos-sized problem?** If the treasure is photo-shaped and free tiers
are too small, the phone-photos chapter's plan (automatic phone backup
and/or a two-drive rotation) can be your copy #3 instead. Free-tier space
is closet-sized, not house-sized — documents fit fine; photos go wherever
your plan says they fit.

## The monthly habit (one minute)

Once a month, glance at the cloud folder from your phone or another
computer:

- files all there? newest files visible?
- sync icon happy?
- (twice a year — see recipe 5 — actually download something.)

Pop that reminder on the worksheet's rhythm list and it stays a
one-minute job for life.

## The two warnings, again because they matter

1. **The cloud folder is a mirror.** Deleting a file here deletes it
   there. So the cloud is copy #3, never the *only* copy of anything —
   drive copy (#2) keeps the versions.
2. **Account, not just files.** Secure the account like it's holding
   your treasures (it is): strong unique password in a password manager,
   two-step sign-in if offered, recovery email still real.

## Check your work

1. From a **different device** (your phone is fine), open the cloud
   folder at the provider's web page.
2. See the files? Open one PDF from there. It displays. Done — that's
   proof the offsite copy exists and works.
3. Write the provider and account name on Worksheet Part 2. (Account
   *name* only — see Part 2's password note.)

## If something's off

- **Files keep not syncing / icon stuck** → check the app's status page:
  often it's the drive being full, the app paused in the background, or a
  conflict copy ("file (conflicted copy)") that needs a look.
- **Free tier full** → either accept the paid tier's price, or move the
  *bulk* item (photos usually) out of plan and into drives (chapter 5's plan).
- **Conflicted copies appeared** → the same file changed in two places
  while apart; open both, keep the one you meant, delete the other —
  calmly, it kept both on purpose.
