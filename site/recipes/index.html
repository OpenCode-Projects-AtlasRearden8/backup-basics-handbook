Recipes: Small Tasks, Done and Checked

Each recipe below is a short task with its steps, a way to check your work, and what to do if something's off. They're written so the checkbox temporarily lives in your brain, then gets checked off on paper for good.

| Recipe | You'll spend | What you end up with | | --- | --- | --- | | Recipe 1 — Make Your First Real Copy | 10–20 min | Treasure folder on an external drive | | Recipe 2 — Prove You Can Restore | 15 min | A logged restore drill and actual confidence | | Recipe 3 — Turn On Automatic Backups | 10 min | Your OS guarding you while you sleep | | Recipe 4 — Put a Folder in the Cloud | 10 min | Copy #3 living offsite, hands-free | | Recipe 5 — The Yearly Archive Check | 30 min, yearly | Files verified openable, names fixed, formats migrated |

Honesty note, before you read further: the shell commands in recipes 1, 2 and 5 have been run end to end in a test shell (recipe 2 includes a checksum comparison, but you don't have to use that part); the click-by-click GUI recipes 3 and 4 follow each operating system's official steps, but menu names drift as versions change — if a menu looks different, trust the surrounding words ("backup," "sync," "turn on") over the exact wording here.
